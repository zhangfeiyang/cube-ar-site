Cube AR — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Cube AR (the "App") is a Rubik's Cube scanning and solving guide. The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

Camera usage

  • The App requests camera access solely to detect the colors of your Rubik's Cube in real time.
  • All camera frames are processed on your device only. No image or video ever leaves the device.
  • The App contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs and makes no network requests.

No data collection

  • No account is required. No personal information is collected.
  • Scan results and settings exist only in memory while the App runs; nothing is stored or shared.

Children

The App contains no chat, social features, or user-generated content sharing, and is safe for all ages.

《Cube AR》隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 16 日

Cube AR（以下简称"本应用"）是一款魔方扫描与还原指导应用。本应用不收集、不存储、不传输、不分享任何个人数据。

相机使用说明

  • 本应用请求相机权限仅用于实时识别你手中魔方的颜色。
  • 所有相机画面仅在你的设备本地处理，任何图像或视频都不会离开设备。
  • 本应用不包含任何统计、追踪或广告 SDK，不发起任何网络请求。

零数据收集

  • 无需注册账号，不收集任何个人信息。
  • 扫描结果与设置仅在本应用运行时存在于内存中，不做任何存储或分享。

儿童

本应用不含聊天、社交或用户内容分享功能，适合所有年龄段。
